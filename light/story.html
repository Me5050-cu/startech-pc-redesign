Every line on this page comes from the shop’s own websites over the years and its Facebook page today.

  1. 1991

    Star Tech opens in Austin

    The shop’s own sites have said it ever since: serving Austin since 1991. In 2001 the home page put it as “Personal Computer Upgrades, Repairs, New and Used Systems, Software, and Games for over Ten Years in Austin.”

    Source: startechpc.com, 2001 and 2006–2017

  2. 2001

    9222 Burnet Rd, next to Kinko’s

    Back then the store was Star Tech Computers, a block north of 183 — already on the same phone number, (512) 719-GAME. The pitch on the home page: “Do you have PC hardware or software collecting dust??? Sell it or trade it!!!”

    Source: startechpc.com, March 2001

  3. 2002

    Moving to 9513 Burnet Rd

    By 2002 Star Tech PC was “located at 9513 Burnet Rd. just north of 183”, under red STAR TECH PC letters — the same address it’s at today. Custom builds were already on the menu: “Build Your Own System!!! We’ll have it ready for you TODAY.”

    Source: startechpc.com, 2002

  4. 2003

    A second store with an internet cafe

    For a while there were two Star Tech stores. The Central location on Guadalupe had an internet cafe for LAN parties, and the shop did onsite service and networking for homes and offices.

    Source: startechpc.com, 2003

  5. 2013

    iPhone Repair Gods

    Its sister store on W. Anderson Lane focused on Apple repair — the name you can still see on the Burnet Rd banner and in the shop’s Facebook handle. Its promise: “We welcome and encourage customers to observe our repair processes.” The Anderson Lane store later merged into Burnet.

    Source: iphonerepairgods.com, 2013; startechpc.com, 2017; Facebook

  6. Today

    One shop on Burnet, open every day

    “Serving Austin locals since 1991. Our services include 1hr iPhone repair, gaming desktops, and laptop repair!” Open every day from 10 to 7, no appointment necessary.

    Source: Facebook; startechpc.com, 2025
